# How Agencies Do MMM Today

*Research date: 2026-08-01. Based on deep-research workflow + vendor pages.*

## Service Delivery

- Large agencies: in-house econometrics/analytics teams + custom models (frequent, expensive)
- Mid agencies: outsourced to measurement consultants (Recast, consulting shops) or vendor SaaS (Measured/Northbeam/Triple Whale)
- Small agencies: spreadsheets + platform in-platform ROAS; MMM rarely used
- Typical turnaround for a consultant MMM: 4-8 weeks. SaaS: hours-to-days.

## Pricing Models

- Flat retainer (monthly, most common for SaaS)
- Per-report / per-model-train (usage-based)
- % of managed ad spend (rare, value-based)
- Enterprise: annual contract + implementation fees

## Client Deliverables

- Channel contribution % and ROAS attribution
- Response / saturation curves (diminishing returns)
- Budget allocation recommendations
- Executive summary PDF + quarterly review deck
- Forecast / scenario planning

## Pain Points (adoption drivers)

1. **Data collection complexity** — stitching spend/impressions/revenue from 6+ platforms
2. **Model distrust** — black-box models; no explainability
3. **Slow turnaround** — 4-8 week consultant cycles vs. need for monthly/quarterly
4. **Cost** — agency-grade tools priced for enterprise brands
5. **Client education** — selling MMM results to non-technical clients

## Feature Priorities (ranked for adoption)

1. One-click data connectors (CSV + platform APIs)
2. Budget scenario planner (interactive what-if)
3. NL insights + executive reports
4. Explainable diagnostics (R-hat, R², curves)
5. Multi-client workspaces + white-label
6. Industry benchmark priors
7. Incrementality/lift-test calibration
8. Team roles + audit log
9. PDF report export
10. API access (for agency internal tooling)

## Positioning Message

"Self-serve Bayesian MMM with AI insights — built for agencies managing many clients. Connect data, train in minutes, get NL budget recommendations. No consultants, no 8-week waits, no per-client enterprise pricing."
